Md Hasibur Rahman, Ph.D.

Ph.D. in Computer Science · Missouri University of Science and Technology

Research: computer vision, multi-object tracking, multimodal sensing, reliable AI, and biomedical AI.

I develop learning methods for dynamic systems that must reason from incomplete, noisy, and heterogeneous observations, with emphasis on reliable spatiotemporal inference.

Research Focus

  • Reliable perception

    Small-object detection and tracking under missed observations, occlusion, motion uncertainty, and clutter.

  • Multimodal sensing

    Fusion of visual, temporal, spatial, and heterogeneous sensor evidence under missing or conflicting observations.

  • Biomedical AI

    Personalized and multimodal learning from physiological, behavioral, imaging, and wearable-sensing data.
