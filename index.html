Md Hasibur Rahman

Computer Vision · Spatiotemporal Learning · Multimodal Sensing · Biomedical AI

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Clemson University, working with Prof. Adam Hoover. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Missouri University of Science and Technology in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Sanjay Madria.

My research focuses on learning from dynamic and multimodal data, with work spanning small-object detection, multi-object tracking, motion and state estimation, sensor fusion, and LiDAR-based 3D localization. My current postdoctoral interests extend this work toward wearable sensing, multimodal time-series learning, human activity and event detection, and biomedical AI.

Research

Computer Vision & Tracking

Wearable & Biomedical AI

Multimodal time-series learning, wearable sensing, and human activity and event detection for biomedical applications.
