I develop learning methods for systems that must reason from incomplete, noisy, and heterogeneous observations. My work spans computer vision, multi-object tracking, multimodal sensing, and emerging biomedical AI, with an emphasis on reliable spatiotemporal inference.

Research

Research themes

Reliable perception

Detection and tracking under small targets, missed observations, occlusion, camera motion, and uncertain dynamics.

Multimodal sensing

Combining visual, temporal, spatial, and heterogeneous sensor evidence while accounting for disagreement and missing data.

Biomedical AI

Extending multimodal and personalized learning to physiological, behavioral, imaging, and wearable-sensing data.

Spatiotemporal learning

Modeling motion, temporal context, structured interactions, and uncertainty across dynamic systems.
